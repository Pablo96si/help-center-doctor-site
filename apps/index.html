Apps

Zendesk apps that run inside your account

Every app here is a client-side Zendesk app: it runs in the agent's browser with the agent's own session, has no server of ours and no vendor account, and is priced per account, not per agent. Every app is installed from the Zendesk Marketplace and billed by Zendesk, with a 14-day trial on paid plans. Each card links to its Marketplace listing, or says so while a listing is still in review.

What they have in common

The same rules for every app

Nothing leaves your account

No backend, no third-party service, no analytics. Reads and writes go through the Zendesk API from your browser with your own permissions. What the apps remember stays in your browser or in the app's own settings inside your account.

Every write is previewed and reversible

Changes are shown before they happen. Help center edits take a snapshot first and have undo; destructive ticket actions need a typed confirmation and are for admins only.

One price per account

Shown on the listing, billed by Zendesk with your subscription, 14-day trial on paid plans, cancel from the Marketplace. Any number of agents.

Questions before you install?

One developer answers every message within one business day.
