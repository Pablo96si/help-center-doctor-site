How Help Center Doctor compares

Three apps on the Zendesk Marketplace cover parts of the same job. The facts below about them are what their listings and, where noted, their vendor pricing pages showed on 5 September 2026. They are good products with real customers; the differences are about approach, scope and price. Check the listings for current details.

If you want an all-in-one managed service

Swifteq Help Center Manager lists more than we do: media library, task management, analytics, AI alt text and automatic translation, with 40 five-star reviews. It runs as a hosted application with a Swifteq account and is priced per article record. Help Center Doctor keeps everything in your browser, shows its price on the listing and prices per account, but does not offer analytics, translation or a media library.

If you only need detection

Lotus Themes Help Center Health Check detects link, image, alt text and structure problems across brands for $29 per month and links to the editor. Help Center Doctor's Free plan covers detection with 34 rules for up to 200 articles, and Pro at the same $29 adds fixing, find and replace, bulk actions, documents and templates for one brand.

If you only need PDF manuals

Swifteq Articles PDF Export builds PDF manuals with custom cover pages, full-text filters and multi-brand support for EUR 49 per month or as part of Help Center Manager. Help Center Doctor's Pro plan includes print / PDF, HTML, Word and Markdown for one brand, built in your browser, alongside the other modules.
